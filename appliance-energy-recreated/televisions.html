Television Energy Tips

Size and display technology can affect electricity consumption. Modern LED/LCD and OLED televisions have different power characteristics, so it is useful to consider the energy label alongside screen size.

  • Use the TV's Energy Saver or Eco mode and reduce brightness where comfortable.
  • Disable always-listening voice features if you do not use them.
  • Consider both star rating and annual kWh on the energy label when comparing models.

Conclusion

When choosing a TV, consider screen size, display technology, annual energy consumption and star rating together. A larger screen can use more power, but efficiency also varies between individual models.
